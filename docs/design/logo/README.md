# ShoeStepper logo concepts

Open `preview.html` to compare. Canvas 108x108, art inside 21..87, groups `background` / `foreground`. Flat fills only.
Colour tokens: Navy `#1A253D` (night backdrop top), Ink `#1E2230`, Purple `#7942DE` (accent 1), Night text `#E8EDF6`, Night accentInk `#C4A6FF`, Light backdrop `#D1DDEC`.

1. 01 sneaker-profile: side-view sneaker, purple sole on navy. The plainest, most direct "shoe".
2. 02 tread-print: sole print with chevron tread bands, white on purple. A shoe you can only know by its mark.
3. 03 footprints: three alternating prints climbing a diagonal path. Says "steps" without a shoe.
4. 04 shoe-on-stairs: sneaker standing on a raised step. Steps plus shoe in one image.
5. 05 shoe-nfc-waves: sneaker with three NFC arcs above the toe. The tap-a-tag idea.
6. 06 nfc-tag-shoe: round tag disc with a shoe cut out. The physical tag you stick in a shoe.
7. 07 shoelace-bow: a tied lace bow. Friendly, calm; no shoe outline to draw wrong.
8. 08 route-shoe: one route line tracing a shoe, dotted track and end pins. Distance as a path.
9. 09 pair-of-shoes: two sneakers, one behind the other. "Per pair" is the app's core idea.
10. 10 monogram-s: stair-cornered letter S. Boldest at 48px, least literal.

Notes: 02 uses a clipPath (gaps), 06 a mask (shoe cut-out), 09 a bg-coloured outline; flatten these to plain paths when converting to a vector drawable.
